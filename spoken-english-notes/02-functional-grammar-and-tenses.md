## 1. Day 08: Prepositions of Place & Time (In, On, At)
Remember the **Inverted Pyramid**:

1. **IN (General / Largest Scope):**
   - **Time:** Centuries, Years, Months (*in 2026, in December, in summer*)
   - **Place:** Countries, Cities, Enclosed spaces (*in India, in Mumbai, in the room, in a car*)
2. **ON (Specific Days & Flat Surfaces):**
   - **Time:** Days & Dates (*on Monday, on my birthday, on 15th August*)
   - **Place:** Surfaces, Streets, Walkable Transit (*on the table, on MG Road, on the bus, on the train*)
3. **AT (Exact Pinpoint):**
   - **Time:** Precise Clock Time (*at 5:00 PM, at midnight, at noon*)
   - **Place:** Exact Addresses, Specific Spots (*at home, at the door, at the airport*)

> **Transit Rule:** If you can stand up and walk inside the vehicle &rarr; use **ON** (*on the bus/train/flight*). If you must crouch inside &rarr; use **IN** (*in the car/taxi*).

## 2. Day 09: Pronouns & Ownership
- **With a Noun (Adjective):** *"This is **my** laptop." / "That is **her** phone."*
- **Standalone at End (Pronoun):** *"This laptop is **mine**." / "The decision is **hers**."*

---

## 3. Day 10 & 11: The 4 Essential Parts of Speech
1. **Noun (चीज़ / नाम):** What is it? (*laptop, tea, Bengaluru*)
2. **Verb (काम):** What action is taking place? (*codes, runs, discusses*)
3. **Adjective (खासियत):** What kind of thing is it? (*fast, smart, exhausted*)
4. **Adverb (तरीका):** How was it done? (*quickly, politely, smoothly*)

---

## 4. Day 12: Expressing Likes, Dislikes & Preferences
- **Love it:** *"I am really into machine learning."*
- **Polite Dislike:** *"It's not really my cup of tea."*
- **Preference:** *"I prefer tea over coffee." / "I'd rather stay home tonight."*
- **Opinion:** *"In my perspective, daily practice beats theory."*

---

## 5. Day 13: Polite Requests (Could You & Would You)
- Instead of *"Give me the file"*, say: **"Could you please share the file?"**
- Instead of *"Open the window"*, say: **"Would you mind opening the window?"**
  *(Polite reply to 'Would you mind': "Not at all, happy to help!")*

## 6. Day 14 & 15: Tenses Simplified for Speaking
### Present Tense:
- **Simple Present (Habits/Facts):** *"I drink coffee every morning."*
- **Present Continuous (Right Now):** *"I am drinking coffee right now."*
- *Stative Verbs rule:* Never say *"I am knowing/understanding"*. Say **"I know"** / **"I understand"**.

### Past & Future:
- **Simple Past (V2):** *"I **went** to the store yesterday." / "She **called** me."*
- **Future:**
  - **Will:** Instant decision (*"I will help you with that."*)
  - **Going to:** Pre-planned action (*"I am going to visit my hometown this weekend."*)
