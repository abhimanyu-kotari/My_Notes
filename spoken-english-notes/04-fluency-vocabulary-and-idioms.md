# Volume 4: Fluency, Vocabulary, Pronunciation & Idioms (Days 23–30)
*Course: Complete Spoken English for Beginners by Udisha Mishra*

---

## 1. Day 23: Expressing Emotions & Nuance
Replace basic words with expressive vocabulary:
- Very happy &rarr; **Thrilled / Delighted**
- Very tired &rarr; **Exhausted / Drained**
- Annoyed &rarr; **Frustrated**
- Relieved &rarr; **"What a relief!"**

---

## 2. Day 24: Multiplying Vocabulary Fast (Root Words)
Learn 1 root word to unlock multiple vocabulary items:
- **BENE (Good):** *Benefit, Beneficial, Benefactor*
- **DICT (Speak):** *Predict, Contradict, Dictate*
- **PORT (Carry):** *Transport, Export, Portable*

> **The Substitution Trick:** If you forget a complicated word like *'enormous'*, don't freeze! Just say *'very big'*. Keep the flow going.

---

## 3. Day 25: Master English Pronunciation & Vowel Sounds
- **Short vs. Long Vowels:**
  - *Ship* (जहाज) vs. *Sheep* (भेड़) &rarr; Stretch the 'ee' sound!
  - *Fit* (तंदुरुस्त) vs. *Feet* (पैर)
  - *Live* (रहना) vs. *Leave* (छोड़ना)
- **Silent Letters:**
  - Silent B: *Doubt, Plumber, Comb*
  - Silent K: *Know, Knee, Knife*
  - Silent L: *Calm, Talk, Should*

## 4. Day 26: 15 Common Indian-English Fixes
| Avoid Saying ❌ | Say This Instead ✅ |
| :--- | :--- |
| "I will revert back to you." | **"I will get back / reply to you."** |
| "Can we prepone the meeting?" | **"Can we advance / move up the meeting?"** |
| "He is my cousin brother." | **"He is my cousin."** |
| "I passed out of college in 2024." | **"I graduated from college in 2024."** |
| "What is your good name?" | **"May I know your name, please?"** |

---

## 5. Day 28: 30 Common Native Idioms
- **A piece of cake:** Very easy.
- **Under the weather:** Feeling sick or unwell.
- **Bite the bullet:** Face a tough situation bravely.
- **Break a leg:** Good luck!
- **Burn the midnight oil:** Working late into the night.

---

## 6. Day 29 & 30: The 15-Minute Daily Fluency Blueprint
1. **01–05 Min (Mirror Speaking):** Speak out loud about your day in front of a mirror.
2. **06–10 Min (Shadowing):** Watch a podcast clip, pause after each sentence, and repeat in the exact rhythm.
3. **11–15 Min (Thinking in English):** Silently narrate what you see around you in English instead of your native tongue.
