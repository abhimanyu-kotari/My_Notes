# Volume 3: Conversational Flow & Modal Superpowers (Days 16–22)
*Course: Complete Spoken English for Beginners by Udisha Mishra*

---

## 1. Day 16: Has, Have, Do, Does Usage
- **Team Singular (He / She / It):** Uses **HAS** & **DOES**
  - *"He **has** a meeting."*
  - *"She **doesn't like** spicy food."* *(Note: When 'does' appears, the main verb drops the 's'!)*
- **Team Plural + I/You (I / You / We / They):** Uses **HAVE** & **DO**
  - *"I **have** a laptop."*
  - *"They **do not know** the address."*

---

## 2. Day 17: Active vs. Passive Voice in Daily Speech
Use Passive Voice when the **result or object** matters more than the doer:
- *"My wallet **was stolen**."* (Doer is unknown)
- *"The project **has been completed**."* (The achievement is what counts)
- *"You **are invited** to the dinner."* (Polite social invitation)

---

## 3. Day 18: All Modal Verbs (The Certainty Scale)
- **MUST (100% Compulsion):** *"You must wear a helmet."*
- **SHOULD (Advice / Recommendation):** *"You should take some rest."*
- **CAN / COULD (Ability & Polite Request):** *"I can solve this." / "Could you assist me?"*
- **MAY / MIGHT (30% - 50% Possibility):** *"It might rain this evening."*

---

## 4. Day 19: 50 Daily Situational Phrases
- **Polite Interruption:** *"Sorry to cut you off, but I had a quick point."*
- **Running Late:** *"I am running a few minutes behind schedule."*
- **Full Agreement:** *"You took the words right out of my mouth!"*
- **Ending a Call:** *"I'll let you get back to your work. Talk soon!"*

---

## 5. Day 20: Subject-Verb Agreement
- **Rule:** Singular Subject = Singular Verb (with -s); Plural Subject = Plural Verb (no -s).
- ❌ *"One of my friend are coming."* &rarr; ✅ **"One of my friends is coming."**
- ❌ *"He don't know."* &rarr; ✅ **"He doesn't know."**
- ❌ *"Everyone are happy."* &rarr; ✅ **"Everyone is happy."**

---

## 6. Day 21 & 22: Time-Shifting & Real Dialogue Drills
Practice the same verb across time:
1. Routine: *"I work from home on Fridays."*
2. Right Now: *"I am working on the presentation right now."*
3. Yesterday: *"I worked until 9 PM yesterday."*
4. Experience: *"I have worked with this technology before."*
5. Tomorrow: *"I will work on testing tomorrow."*
