A Letter to You: You Do NOT "Suck" at English!

If you forget words or get stuck in the middle of a sentence, remember: This is completely normal. Your brain is trying to translate from your mother tongue into English word-by-word, which creates mental traffic jams!

TIP 01

Fluency Over Grammar

Don't worry about making mistakes. Even native English speakers speak imperfect grammar every single day! Confidence matters first.

TIP 02

Learn in Chunks

Instead of memorizing dictionary words, memorize 2-to-3 word blocks (e.g. "Could you please...", "I was wondering...").

TIP 03

The Substitution Trick

If you forget a difficult word like "enormous", just say "very big"! Never pause, freeze, or panic.
